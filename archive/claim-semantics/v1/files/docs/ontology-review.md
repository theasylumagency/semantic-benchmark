# Claim Semantics — ontology decisions and consistency audit

2026-09-27. This review follows [`factualAssertion.md`](factualAssertion.md) and [`Cases.md`](Cases.md). The seed alignment below preceded provider runs. The six decisions in this document were supplied explicitly by the benchmark owner. **No Jev or GPT-6 Sol output was used as gold authority.** Resolving an ontology question leaves the case in `draft`; it does not mark it human-reviewed.

## `factualAssertion` boundary

`factualAssertion` asks whether the brand asserts a descriptive proposition that can in principle be checked. It does not ask whether a public claim requires proof. The two contracts are scored separately. A current negative state such as an ended offer is a factual assertion; a negated superlative or guarantee is not itself that promotional claim.

The seed annotations were aligned before the first real provider run:

| Seed text IDs | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `013`, `044`, `053`, `071`, `075`–`080` | `factualAssertion = NO` | Best, leading, flexible, lowest, or top-rank language lacks the defined measurable basis required for a descriptive fact. Other claim contracts can still be `YES`. |
| `017`, `019`, `021`, `023`, `039`, `081`–`086` | `factualAssertion = NO` | These are guarantees, refund/service promises, or future individual outcomes. They remain positive where the guarantee or clinical contract applies. |
| `020`, `047` | `factualAssertion = YES` | A no-guarantee policy and an ended promotion are verifiable current states even though phrased negatively. |

The precise per-item rationale is generated by `scripts/generate_dataset.py`. Unchanged comparative duration and present-tense population outcome statements remain factual when they describe a testable relationship rather than promise each reader a future result. A reviewer should revisit this distinction if evidence or the canonical definition changes.

## Resolved gold and canonical rules

Seed group `ka-text-054` was moved from validation to development before the first provider run. It remains in development after resolution. All six formerly excluded rows below now have boolean gold and `goldStatus = draft`.

| Row | Decision | Rule and rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ka-claim-054-clinicalOutcomeClaim` | **YES** | “მკურნალობა შეიძლება დაგეხმაროთ ტკივილის შემცირებაში.” names pain reduction. **Hedging changes certainty, not claim type.** |
| `ka-claim-054-proofRequirement` | **YES** | A qualified public clinical outcome claim still requires support. |
| `ka-exp-002-quantifiedClaim` | **NO** | “ადგილები აღარ დარჩა.” states availability. Do not infer an unstated numeric zero from the state. |
| `ka-exp-005-clinicalOutcomeClaim` | **NO** | “პირველი ვიზიტიდანვე იგრძნობთ განსხვავებას.” does not name a clinical endpoint, symptom, functional improvement, recovery change, or other defined result. |
| `ka-exp-005-guaranteeClaim` | **NO** | The same neutral “difference” is too vague to meet the guarantee contract’s clear-result rule. No new outcome contract is introduced. |
| `ka-exp-010-priceClaim` | **YES** | “ოქტომბრიდან კონსულტაცია 150 ლარი ეღირება.” asserts a specific future price. **Temporal validity changes context, not semantic claim type.** |

The future-price decision separates *price-claim type* from *when that price applies* conceptually. A temporal subsystem is not needed for this benchmark change and has not been built. The existing `discountClaim` active-offer rule is a separate contract and was not changed here.

## Development and validation consistency audit

The audit checked all 139 unique texts across both splits, including the following close cases. Existing labels were retained where their wording has a relevant distinction.

| Rule | Comparable cases and conclusion |
| --- | --- |
| Hedged clinical outcome | Development `ka-claim-042-clinicalOutcomeClaim`, `ka-claim-087-clinicalOutcomeClaim`, and `ka-exp-012-clinicalOutcomeClaim` already say **YES** to named improvement, swelling reduction, or another patient outcome. `ka-claim-022` only discusses possible outcomes during a consultation; `ka-claim-043` promises an explanation of recovery duration. Their clinical **NO** labels remain consistent because neither asserts a treatment result. |
| Unstated numeric zero | Added `ka-claim-050-quantifiedClaim = NO` for “დღეს მიღებაზე ადგილი აღარ გვაქვს.” while preserving its four existing rows. Validation `ka-claim-091-quantifiedClaim` (all times booked) and `ka-claim-094-quantifiedClaim` (last bookable visit) were already **NO**. Explicitly counted places in development `ka-claim-090-quantifiedClaim` and validation `ka-claim-029-quantifiedClaim` remain **YES**. |
| Vague “difference” | `ka-exp-005` is **NO** for clinical outcome and guarantee. `ka-claim-021` says “შედეგს … იგრძნობთ,” and `ka-adv-030` says “უკეთ იგრძნობთ თავს”; these assert a result or positive patient-state change rather than a directionless difference, so their clinical **YES** labels remain. Direct guarantee language in `ka-claim-017`, `ka-claim-084`, and `ka-adv-028` remains distinct from the unsupported inference of a guarantee from “difference.” |
| Price time and attribution | The future price in `ka-exp-010` is **YES**. Current/starting prices and the old-and-current price in `ka-claim-007` were already **YES**. The hypothetical `ka-adv-045-priceClaim` and attributed customer price `ka-adv-014-priceClaim` remain **NO** because the brand makes no price assertion in either. |

No other existing gold labels were changed. The new `ka-claim-050-quantifiedClaim` is the only added case. The current ontology-review count is **0**; all resolved and newly added rows still need the ordinary human gold-review workflow before final freeze.
