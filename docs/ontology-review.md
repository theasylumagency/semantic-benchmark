# Claim Semantics — pre-run ontology alignment

2026-09-27. This review follows [`factualAssertion.md`](factualAssertion.md) and [`Cases.md`](Cases.md). No Jev or GPT-6 Sol output was used to set or change these gold labels.

## `factualAssertion` boundary

`factualAssertion` asks whether the brand asserts a descriptive proposition that can in principle be checked. It does not ask whether a public claim requires proof. The two contracts are scored separately. A current negative state such as an ended offer is a factual assertion; a negated superlative or guarantee is not itself that promotional claim.

The seed annotations were aligned before the first real provider run:

| Seed text IDs | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `013`, `044`, `053`, `071`, `075`–`080` | `factualAssertion = NO` | Best, leading, flexible, lowest, or top-rank language lacks the defined measurable basis required for a descriptive fact. Other claim contracts can still be `YES`. |
| `017`, `019`, `021`, `023`, `039`, `081`–`086` | `factualAssertion = NO` | These are guarantees, refund/service promises, or future individual outcomes. They remain positive where the guarantee or clinical contract applies. |
| `020`, `047` | `factualAssertion = YES` | A no-guarantee policy and an ended promotion are verifiable current states even though phrased negatively. |

The precise per-item rationale is generated by `scripts/generate_dataset.py`. Unchanged comparative duration and present-tense population outcome statements remain factual when they describe a testable relationship rather than promise each reader a future result. A reviewer should revisit this distinction if evidence or the canonical definition changes.

## Unresolved gold

Seed group `ka-text-054` says a treatment *may* help reduce pain. It was moved from holdout to development before any real provider run, because an unresolved ontology case must not be blind final-test gold. Its `clinicalOutcomeClaim` and `proofRequirement` labels are `UNCERTAIN` with `goldStatus = ontology-review`. Both are excluded from quality and calibration metrics until the ontology question is resolved in writing. Clear negative price and discount rows for the same text remain scoreable.

After the first development comparison, three **new expansion** annotations were independently checked against the contract definitions and moved to ontology review; none of the 48 user-supplied Batch 01 gold labels was changed:

| Expansion row | Unresolved question | Reason to exclude now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ka-exp-002-quantifiedClaim` | Does a stated absence of remaining slots count as an implicit numeric zero? | `quantifiedClaim` does not yet specify whether implicit zero counts as an asserted quantity. |
| `ka-exp-005-clinicalOutcomeClaim` | Is “you will feel a difference” a clinical outcome? | No clinical endpoint, symptom, or recovery change is named. |
| `ka-exp-005-guaranteeClaim` | Is that vague assured “difference” a clear guaranteed result? | The guarantee contract requires a clear outcome, but “difference” has no defined direction or content. |

These are ontology questions, not model errors. They must be resolved before freeze, with a documented rule that applies to all affected cases.
